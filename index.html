Physics-Informed Modern Neighborhood Component Attention for Bond Strength Prediction of FRP Bars in Ultra-High-Performance Concrete

Abstract

PI-MNCA is a physics-informed Modern Neighborhood Component Attention regressor for the ultimate pullout bond strength τu of fiber-reinforced polymer (FRP) bars embedded in ultra-high-performance concrete (UHPC). A 128-dimensional learned embedding feeds a soft kernel attention over a fixed memory bank of 380 training specimens. The target is ACI-style normalized as y = ln(τu/√fc), a soft monotonicity penalty enforces four mechanically known directions, and a split-conformal calibration delivers a distribution-free 90% prediction band. Every prediction is traceable to the specific training specimens that supported it.

Method

R1. Modern NCA soft kernel attention. Predictions are a softmax-weighted average of training targets in a learned 128-d embedding, with similarity computed by Euclidean distance: $\hat y(x_q) = \sum_i \mathrm{softmax}_i(-\|f_\theta(x_q)-f_\theta(x_i)\|_2/T)\,\bar y_i$.

R2. ACI-style target normalization. Training targets are transformed to $y=\ln(\tau_u/\sqrt{f_c})$, which is invariant under the proportional scaling $f_c\to\alpha f_c,\ \tau_u\to\sqrt{\alpha}\,\tau_u$ that mirrors the ACI bond model.

R3. Physics-informed monotonicity penalty. A soft penalty suppresses local violations of four mechanically known directions: $f_c\uparrow$, $l/d\downarrow$, $\rho_{SF}\uparrow$, $c/d\uparrow$.

R4. Dimension-symmetry augmentation. Random sign/permutation of the embedding dimensions exploits the geometric invariances of the pullout problem and acts as a regularizer.

R5. Split-conformal calibration. The 90% prediction band is the empirical quantile of held-out residuals, giving valid marginal coverage without distributional assumptions.

R6. Case-level interpretability. Attention weights trace every prediction back to the specific training specimens that supported it, exposing the evidence behind each estimate.
